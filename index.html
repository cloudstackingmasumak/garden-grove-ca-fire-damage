Upholstered Furniture Restoration

Upholstered furniture in Garden Grove homes faces complex restoration challenges because the damage extends through multiple layers. The outer fabric is soiled with soot and smoke. The wooden frames of upholstered furniture in Garden Grove can be damaged by heat and smoke as well. Wood can char on the surface and may need to be sanded and refinished. Professional restoration involves disassembling and inspecting the frame, reinforcing it if necessary, and then reassembling and reupholstering the piece.
